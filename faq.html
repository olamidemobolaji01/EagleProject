Find answers to common questions about our online programs, admissions, tuition and student life.

Is Eagle Generation Bible School accredited?

Eagle Generation Bible School is a practical, ministry-focused training school and is currently unaccredited. Students enroll to answer God's call, prepare for ministry, or simply grow in their walk with the Lord. On completing a program and meeting the requirements, you receive a certificate from Eagle Generation Bible School.

Are the programs online or in-person?

Every program is delivered 100% online with flexible, self-paced study, so you can learn from anywhere in the world at a pace that fits your schedule.

Do I need prior theological training to enroll?

No. Our programs are designed for both new believers and experienced ministers. Each program begins with strong foundations and builds from there.

How long does a program take?

Each course of study is made up of focused lessons, and most certificate programs run from 6 to 12 weeks. You can progress at your own pace within each program.

Do I receive a certificate?

Yes. When you successfully complete a program and meet the requirements, you receive a certificate recognizing your studies at Eagle Generation Bible School.

What does tuition cost?

Tuition is kept affordable and can be arranged in instalments. A small non-refundable application fee may apply. For the current fees for each program, please email study@EagleGenBibleSchool.com.

Do you offer scholarships or financial help?

As a practical training school, most external grants and scholarships do not apply. We do, however, consider church-matching support in certain cases. Contact our admissions team to learn more.

When should I apply and when is payment due?

We recommend submitting your application as early as possible to secure your place in the upcoming intake. Once your non-refundable application fee is received, your application will be reviewed.

Can international students apply?

Yes. Because our programs are fully online, students from anywhere in the world can enroll — no travel or study visa is required to study with us.
