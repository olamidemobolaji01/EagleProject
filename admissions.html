Tuition

Fees and payment

  • Fees are set per intake — email study@EagleGenBibleSchool.com for current fees.
  • Payment is by manual bank transfer. We do not take card payments and no card data is stored.
  • Instalments are available — tell us when you apply and we will arrange a schedule.
  • The application fee is non-refundable.
