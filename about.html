Our Story

Raising a generation that soars

Eagle Generation Bible School is the training arm of the International Gathering of Eagles Ministries (IGOE), a global ministry raised up to train and equip church leaders and believers for effective leadership, church growth and Spirit-filled service.

From our home in North York, Ontario, we equip students online across the nations, offering focused certificate programs that carry the same mandate: to know the Word, live the Word and lead.

Our Ministry

The International Gathering of Eagles Ministries

The International Gathering of Eagles (IGOE) is an annual conference and global ministry established in 2003 through God's servant, Pastor Amos Dele Dada, Senior Pastor of Christ Apostolic Church Bethel, Toronto, Canada.

Headquartered in Toronto, IGOE is devoted to training and equipping church leaders and Christians in their various callings, building godly character, sound leadership and the impartation of the power of the Holy Spirit.

Through conferences, seminars, church planting, crusades and outreaches, the ministry has reached over twenty nations across seven regions of the world, propagating the name of Jesus Christ to the ends of the earth. Eagle Generation Bible School continues that vision by equipping the next generation.

What we believe

Statement of Faith

  • The Bible is the inspired, infallible and authoritative Word of God.
  • There is one God, eternally existent in three persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit.
  • Jesus Christ is the Son of God, born of a virgin, crucified, risen and coming again.
  • Salvation is by grace through faith in Christ alone, and not by works.
  • The Holy Spirit indwells, empowers and gifts the believer for life and service.
  • The Church is the body of Christ, called to make disciples of all nations.
