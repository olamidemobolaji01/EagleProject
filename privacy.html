Privacy Policy

How Eagle Generation Bible School collects, uses and protects the information you give us.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when you submit an application or inquiry, create an account, or enrol in a program, such as your name, email, phone number, address and program preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process applications, deliver courses, track your learning progress, communicate with you about admissions and tuition, and improve our services.

3. Email Communications

We send transactional emails such as application confirmations and approval notices. We will never ask you for your password or payment card details by email.

4. Payment Information

Tuition is paid manually by bank transfer. We do not collect or store credit card numbers on this website. Payment records we keep relate only to amounts received against your tuition.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Faculty can view the contact details and progress of students enrolled in their own courses solely for teaching and support purposes.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Passwords are stored in hashed form and access to student and applicant data is restricted to authorised staff.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us.
